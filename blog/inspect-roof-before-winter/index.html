Why Inspect Your Roof Before Winter

By Utilisight Corp·Nov 07, 2026·X min read

Winter is hard on commercial roofs: snow loads, ice, wind and repeated freeze-thaw cycles. Going into winter with trapped moisture under the membrane can turn a small repair into a bigger one.

How freeze-thaw makes moisture worse

Water expands by about 9% when it freezes. Moisture that entered the roof in summer may sit unnoticed. Once temperatures drop below freezing, it can:

  • Stress the membrane and seams as it expands.
  • Damage insulation through repeated freezing and thawing, reducing its insulating value.
  • Speed up deck deterioration, such as corrosion on steel or decay of wood.

A small wet area that could have been repaired in the fall may need larger repairs by spring.

Benefits of a pre-winter scan

  • Avoid winter emergency repairs. Repairs in freezing weather cost more, and some materials are harder to install in the cold.
  • Reduce heat loss. Wet insulation lets more heat escape. Replacing it can help lower heating costs.
  • Protect interior space. Snowmelt finds weak points and can damage equipment, inventory and finishes.

Plan before the first snowfall

A late-fall thermal scan gives you time to investigate and repair suspected wet areas before snow covers the roof.
