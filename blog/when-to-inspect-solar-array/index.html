When to Inspect Your Solar Array

By Utilisight Corp·Nov 07, 2026·X min read

Visual checks matter, but a thermal inspection shows what the eye can't: panels and connections that may not be performing. Timing it well helps protect production, warranty options and the value of the system.

1. At commissioning or handover

The best time to set a baseline is when the system is new.

  • Installation damage: cells cracked during transport or handling are often invisible to the eye but may show in thermal.
  • Connections: hot connectors or offline strings can be flagged before the system goes into regular operation.
  • A baseline: gives you a reference to compare against future inspections.
  • Installer follow-up: gives you documentation to raise issues with the installer before final sign-off.

2. Before warranties end

Solar systems usually carry several warranties. Terms vary by manufacturer and contract, so check your own agreements for exact dates.

Warranty Covers Typical term
Installer workmanship Installation, wiring and connections Varies by contract
Panel product Manufacturing defects such as diodes, junction boxes and glass Often 10–25 years
Panel performance Power output over time Often 25–30 years

A scan 6 to 12 months before a warranty ends leaves time to document issues and file a claim.

  • Support a claim: thermal images document suspected defects. Manufacturers usually also require their own testing.
  • Catch slow-developing issues: PID and delamination can take years to appear.
  • Support a sale or refinancing: an independent report documents the array's condition for buyers or lenders.

3. After severe weather

Hail, high winds and lightning can damage panels in ways you can't see.

  • Hidden cracks: hail can crack cells while the glass looks intact. Cracked cells may show as hot spots while the panels are producing.
  • Racking and wiring: wind can shift hardware and strain wiring, causing faults or offline strings.
  • Insurance: geolocated thermal and visual images help document damage for a claim.
