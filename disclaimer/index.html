1. Use of the term “survey”

The words “survey” and “thermographic survey” on this Website describe aerial thermal and visual data capture. They do not refer to legal, cadastral or boundary surveys regulated by the Association of Ontario Land Surveyors (AOLS). Utilisight does not offer legal land surveys or services intended to establish or define property boundaries; those can only be performed by a licensed Ontario Land Surveyor.

2. Findings are observational

Our reports document thermal and visual conditions observed at the time of the flight. Findings such as “may indicate” or “may be consistent with” are observations that point to areas for follow-up investigation. They are not a diagnosis of cause, a guarantee of condition or a prediction of failure.

3. Not a substitute for professional services

Our reports do not replace the work of licensed professionals. They are not an engineering, structural, electrical-safety or code-compliance certification, a roofing or product warranty determination, an insurance assessment, or a legal survey. Decisions about repairs, safety and claims should be made with the appropriate qualified professional.

4. Conditions and limitations

Thermal results depend on weather, time of day, surface conditions, load and access at the time of capture. Areas that cannot be seen or measured, such as roof areas beneath solar panels, are not assessed thermally. Accuracy values and detection figures are indicative and apply under suitable conditions.

5. Illustrative content

Images, sample reports and figures on this Website are illustrative examples and may not reflect the results of a specific project.

6. Questions

For questions about this disclaimer or about the scope of our services, please contact us or write to ralvares@utilisight.ca.
