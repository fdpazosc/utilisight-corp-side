The tools behind every inspection

Radiometric thermal camera

Temperature data in every pixel, so findings can be measured, compared and ranked.

  • 640×512 radiometric sensor
  • Detects differences of 0.05 °C
  • ±3 °C or ±3% accuracy
  • RTK positioning, GPS-tagged frames

Structured reports

Clear findings your team or contractor can act on.

  • Findings catalog with severity ratings
