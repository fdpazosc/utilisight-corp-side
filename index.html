The problem

Traditional inspections are slow, costly, and dangerous

Hidden roof moisture, failing connections and underperforming panels often go unnoticed until they become costly repairs.

Time inefficiency

Walking a roof or climbing a structure covers a fraction of the area a drone captures in a single flight.

High operational costs

Thermal screening shows where to focus, so physical testing and repairs are targeted rather than broad.
