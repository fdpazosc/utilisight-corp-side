Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Utilisight Corp (“Utilisight”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through utilisight.ca (the “Website”). It complements our Terms and Conditions.

1. Who is responsible

Utilisight Corp is responsible for the personal information collected through the Website. Registered address: [company legal name and address]. Privacy contact: ralvares@utilisight.ca.

2. Information we collect

When you submit the inspection request form we collect:

  • Full name, company and work email address;
  • Asset type, and optionally asset size, target window and scope notes.

Our hosting provider may also record technical data such as your IP address and browser type in standard server logs.

3. How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare a scope and quote, schedule the work and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Website and deliver your request when required by law. These providers may process data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

5. How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for [retention period, e.g. 12 months] or as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain our business records, and then delete or anonymize them.

6. Security

The Website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), access to submissions is limited to authorized people, and automated filters help block spam. No system is completely secure, and sending information over the Internet always involves some risk.

7. Cookies and analytics

The Website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to its use, by writing to ralvares@utilisight.ca. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Third-party links

The Website links to third-party services such as Instagram. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The current version is always available at this address.

11. Applicable law

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada’s anti-spam legislation (CASL), as well as applicable Ontario law.
