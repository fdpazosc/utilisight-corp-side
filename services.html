Inspection services

Thermographic surveys

Heat, turned into evidence

Radiometric thermal capture identifies hotspots and abnormal heating patterns across electrical and energy assets. Every finding is geolocated, image-linked and prioritized for action.

Key detection capabilities

  • Overheating connectors and breakers
  • Load imbalance indications
  • Thermal anomalies in modules and strings
  • Insulation and moisture signatures
  • Early-stage component failures
  • Repeatable baselines for trend analysis
